## [Auditory Closed-Loop Stimulation of the Sleep Slow Oscillation Enhances Memory](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.03.006)

- **Year / source:** 2013 · Neuron · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Times auditory stimulation to detected sleep slow oscillations to test whether phase-locked closed-loop stimulation changes memory consolidation.
- **Why it matters:** Provides direct evidence about whether sensing-linked feedback, adaptive messaging, automated control or closed-loop stimulation changes behaviour, physiology or clinical outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Effects are specific to the tested population, intervention package, comparator, device, adherence pattern and follow-up window; transportability and long-term safety require separate evidence.
- **Evidence boundary:** Randomization supports the specified trial estimand, but it does not validate every algorithmic component, subgroup policy or future deployment configuration.
