## [A Hybrid EMG–EEG Interface for Robust Intention Detection and Fatigue-Adaptive Control of an Elbow Rehabilitation Robot](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-24831-w)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · Scientific Reports · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `model` · `representation` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Combines peripheral EMG and central EEG to detect movement intention and adapt control when muscle fatigue degrades EMG-only recognition.
- **Why it matters:** Illustrates multimodal wearable control that explicitly models a failure mode—fatigue—rather than assuming stationary sensor quality.
- **Limitations:** The study is a technical evaluation with limited tasks and populations; it does not establish rehabilitation effectiveness, long-term safety or home deployment robustness.
- **Evidence boundary:** This record supports a representation, recognition, sensing or control-system claim under the reported evaluation. It does not by itself establish clinical benefit, causal treatment effect, safety, transportability or regulatory readiness.
