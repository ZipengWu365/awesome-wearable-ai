## [Wearable Device Derived Electrocardiographic Age and Its Association with Atrial Fibrillation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02344-8)

- **Year / source:** 2026 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Derives an AI electrocardiographic age measure from wearable-device ECG and evaluates its association with atrial fibrillation.
- **Why it matters:** Extends ECG-age concepts from clinical 12-lead recordings to lower-burden wearable measurements.
- **Limitations:** Association with AF does not establish incremental clinical utility, causal interpretation or transportability to other wearable ECG hardware.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported relationship is observational or predictive. It does not establish that changing the wearable-derived measure will causally change the clinical outcome.
