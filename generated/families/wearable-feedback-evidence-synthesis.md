## [An Umbrella Review of Systematic Reviews of the Impact of Wrist-Worn Wearables on Health Outcomes](https://doi.org/10.1152/physrev.00049.2024)

- **Year / source:** 2026 · Physiological Reviews · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `systematic-review`
- **Contribution:** Synthesizes 39 systematic reviews covering 98 primary studies of wrist-worn wearable feedback across physical activity, cardiometabolic, quality-of-life, mental-health and pain outcomes.
- **Why it matters:** Provides direct evidence about whether sensing-linked feedback, adaptive messaging, automated control or closed-loop stimulation changes behaviour, physiology or clinical outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Conclusions inherit heterogeneity and risk of bias from included studies; devices, co-interventions, comparators and outcome definitions vary substantially.
- **Evidence boundary:** Evidence synthesis estimates average effects over included studies and does not establish effectiveness for every device, population or intervention design.
- **Verification:** `primary_source_checked` on 2026-09-02
