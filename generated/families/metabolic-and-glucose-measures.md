## [Predicting Changes in Glycemic Control among Adults with Prediabetes from Activity Patterns Collected by Wearable Devices](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-021-00541-1)

- **Year / source:** 2021 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Evaluates whether wrist- and waist-wearable activity patterns improve prediction of changes in HbA1c among adults with prediabetes.
- **Why it matters:** Connects free-living behavior to a clinically familiar metabolic outcome and explicitly compares wearable placement.
- **Limitations:** The analysis predicts change within a trial cohort but does not establish that model-guided behavior change improves glycemic control.
- **Evidence boundary:** This supports analytical or clinical validation in the studied cohort and device setting; it does not establish universal cross-device validity or downstream clinical benefit.
