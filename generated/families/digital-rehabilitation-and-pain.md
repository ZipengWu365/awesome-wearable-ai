## [Telehealth Virtual Reality Intervention Reduces Chronic Pain in a Randomized Crossover Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01553-x)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Evaluates a remotely delivered virtual-reality intervention for chronic pain using a randomized crossover design.
- **Why it matters:** Provides direct evidence about whether sensing-linked feedback, adaptive messaging, automated control or closed-loop stimulation changes behaviour, physiology or clinical outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Effects are specific to the tested population, intervention package, comparator, device, adherence pattern and follow-up window; transportability and long-term safety require separate evidence.
- **Evidence boundary:** Randomization supports the specified trial estimand, but it does not validate every algorithmic component, subgroup policy or future deployment configuration.

## [Utilization of Telemedicine in Conjunction with Wearable Devices for Patients with Chronic Musculoskeletal Pain: A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-85056-x)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · Scientific Reports · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Randomized trial compared six months of home telemedicine plus wearable monitoring and usual care with usual care alone for chronic musculoskeletal pain.
- **Why it matters:** Adds longer-duration evidence for a wearable-enabled remote-care pathway rather than a short engagement study.
- **Limitations:** Seventy-one participants, a bundled intervention and multiple outcomes limit attribution to the wearable component and broad generalization.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported effect is tied to the randomized intervention, comparator, population, follow-up and endpoint; it should not be generalized to other devices or care pathways without new evidence.
- **Verification:** `primary_source_checked` on 2026-09-02
