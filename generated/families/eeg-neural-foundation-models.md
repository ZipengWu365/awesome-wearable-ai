## [REVE: A Foundation Model for EEG - Adapting to Any Setup with Large-Scale Pretraining on 25,000 Subjects](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/20a917f77773ac0fa8bea2bdd6606b66-Abstract-Conference.html)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · NeurIPS · `top_conference`
- **Type / stage:** `model` · `representation` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Pretrains a topology-flexible EEG foundation model on more than 60,000 hours from 92 datasets and approximately 25,000 subjects, then evaluates transfer across ten EEG tasks.
- **Why it matters:** Directly addresses electrode-layout, dataset and protocol heterogeneity, a central obstacle to reusable EEG representations.
- **Limitations:** Reported transfer is limited to the evaluated EEG corpora and tasks; clinical utility, demographic robustness and prospective deployment were not established by the conference study.
- **Evidence boundary:** This is representation-learning or predictive evidence. It does not establish that a sensor-derived prediction is a causal treatment effect, clinically useful decision rule, or safe deployed intervention.
