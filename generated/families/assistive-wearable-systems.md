## [Human-Centred Design and Fabrication of a Wearable Multimodal Visual Assistance System](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01018-6)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · Nature Machine Intelligence · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `prospective-observational`
- **Contribution:** Integrates computer vision, audio, haptics, smart insoles and user training in a wearable visual-assistance system tested with participants with visual impairment.
- **Why it matters:** Broadens wearable AI from passive health prediction to multimodal sensory substitution and assistive action.
- **Limitations:** The study emphasizes technical performance and usability; comparative clinical-effectiveness evidence and long-term adoption remain limited.
- **Evidence boundary:** Participant experiments support system feasibility and usability but do not establish population-level clinical benefit.
