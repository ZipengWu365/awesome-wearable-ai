## [Closed-Loop Vagus Nerve Stimulation Aids Recovery from Spinal Cord Injury](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09028-5)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · Nature · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `closed-loop-deployment` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Double-blind sham-controlled randomized study paired successful sensor-measured rehabilitation movements with implanted vagus nerve stimulation.
- **Why it matters:** Provides direct evidence for a sensor-triggered implanted closed-loop rehabilitation system.
- **Limitations:** Early-feasibility study with 19 participants and an intensive specialized rehabilitation protocol; larger confirmatory trials are needed.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported effect is tied to the randomized intervention, comparator, population, follow-up and endpoint; it should not be generalized to other devices or care pathways without new evidence.
- **Verification:** `primary_source_checked` on 2026-09-02
