## [Association of Physical Activity Pattern and Risk of Parkinson’s Disease](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01135-3)

- **Year / source:** 2024 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Compares concentrated weekend-warrior and regularly distributed accelerometer-derived activity patterns with incident Parkinson disease.
- **Why it matters:** Adds temporal distribution of activity to total-volume digital epidemiology.
- **Limitations:** Activity pattern is observational, incident cases are relatively few, and undiagnosed prodromal disease could influence behavior.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported relationship is observational or predictive. It does not establish that changing the wearable-derived measure will causally change the clinical outcome.
- **Verification:** `primary_source_checked` on 2026-09-02

- **Year / source:** 2023 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`

- **Year / source:** 2018 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Examines how sensor number, placement, participant diversity and repeated assessments affect tremor and bradykinesia detection.
