## [Sensor-Wide Association Studies in Digital Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02821-0)

- **Year / source:** 2026 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `methodological`
- **Contribution:** Proposes Sensor-Wide Association Studies for prespecified, multiplicity-controlled scans of sensor-derived features against clinical phenotypes.
- **Why it matters:** Offers a transparent discovery framework for high-dimensional wearable feature libraries analogous to genome-wide association studies.
- **Limitations:** The perspective defines a research design rather than proving that discovered sensor associations are causal, portable or clinically useful.
- **Evidence boundary:** This is a methodological perspective; association discovery must be followed by replication, causal analysis and validation of intended use.
