## [An Adaptive AI-Based Virtual Reality Sports System for Adolescents with Excess Body Weight: A Randomized Controlled Trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03724-5)

- **Year / source:** 2025 · Nature Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Randomized five-arm evaluation of an adaptive VR sports system whose coaching agents were trained with deep reinforcement learning.
- **Why it matters:** Connects adaptive AI policy learning to an embodied exercise intervention with a clinical body-composition endpoint.
- **Limitations:** The eight-week trial involved adolescents with excess body weight in a structured VR setting; longer-term adherence, safety and transportability require study.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported effect is tied to the randomized intervention, comparator, population, follow-up and endpoint; it should not be generalized to other devices or care pathways without new evidence.

## [Efficacy and Safety of Using Auditory-Motor Entrainment to Improve Walking after Stroke: A Multi-Site Randomized Controlled Trial of InTandem](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-44791-5)

- **Year / source:** 2024 · Nature Communications · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Multi-site randomized trial evaluated an autonomous closed-loop music system that adapts auditory stimulation to an individual's gait pattern.
- **Why it matters:** Shows adaptive sensing and feedback improving a functional rehabilitation endpoint outside a laboratory-only demonstration.
- **Limitations:** The study evaluated chronic post-stroke walking over five weeks; generalization to other impairments and long-term maintenance requires evidence.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported effect is tied to the randomized intervention, comparator, population, follow-up and endpoint; it should not be generalized to other devices or care pathways without new evidence.
- **Verification:** `primary_source_checked` on 2026-09-02
