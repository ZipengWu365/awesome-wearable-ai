## [Randomized Trial of Two Artificial Intelligence Coaching Interventions to Increase Physical Activity in Cancer Survivors](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-021-00539-9)

- **Year / source:** 2021 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Three-arm pilot randomized trial comparing voice-assisted AI coaching, autonomous text coaching and health information, with Fitbit-derived steps as the primary outcome.
- **Why it matters:** Provides direct randomized evidence for an AI coaching system informed by wearable activity data.
- **Limitations:** Single-center pilot with 42 participants, four weeks of intervention and a selected cancer-survivor population; durability and generalizability remain uncertain.
- **Evidence boundary:** The reported effect is tied to the randomized intervention, comparator, population, follow-up and endpoint; it should not be generalized to other devices or care pathways without new evidence.
