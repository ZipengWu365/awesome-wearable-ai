## [Biometrics of Complete Human Pregnancy Recorded by Wearable Devices](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01183-9)

- **Year / source:** 2024 · npj Digital Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `measure` · `measurement` · `external-validation`
- **Contribution:** Characterizes longitudinal wearable physiological and behavioral trajectories across complete pregnancies.
- **Why it matters:** Provides dense within-person reference trajectories for a state that changes faster than routine prenatal visit schedules.
- **Limitations:** Consumer-device measures, cohort selection and limited adverse-outcome counts constrain clinical thresholds and individual prediction.
- **Evidence boundary:** This supports analytical or clinical validation in the studied cohort and device setting; it does not establish universal cross-device validity or downstream clinical benefit.
