## [Smartphone-Based Screening for Atrial Fibrillation: A Pragmatic Randomized Clinical Trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-01979-w)

- **Year / source:** 2022 · Nature Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `intervention` · `clinical-evaluation` · `randomized-controlled-trial`
- **Contribution:** Pragmatic siteless randomized trial comparing smartphone pulse-wave screening plus confirmatory ECG with usual care in 5,551 adults.
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates that a digital sensing-to-confirmation pathway can change detection of treatment-relevant atrial fibrillation at population scale.
- **Limitations:** The sensing modality was smartphone photoplethysmography rather than a continuously worn device, and improved detection was not evidence of improved downstream clinical outcomes.
- **Evidence boundary:** Randomization supports an effect on AF detection under this screening pathway; it does not establish stroke reduction, net benefit or effectiveness of all wearable AF-screening strategies.
