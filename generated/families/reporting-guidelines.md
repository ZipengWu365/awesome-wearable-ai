- **Type / stage:** `infrastructure` · `governance` · `methodological`
- **Contribution:** Extends diagnostic-accuracy reporting guidance for studies that evaluate AI-based index tests.
- **Why it matters:** Relevant to wearable algorithms positioned as detection, screening or diagnostic tools.

- **Type / stage:** `infrastructure` · `governance` · `methodological`
- **Contribution:** Provides reporting guidance for early-stage, live clinical evaluation of AI decision-support systems.
- **Why it matters:** Targets the poorly documented transition from retrospective model evaluation to real clinical workflow use.

## [Guidelines for Clinical Trial Protocols for Interventions Involving Artificial Intelligence: The SPIRIT-AI Extension](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-020-1037-7)

- **Year / source:** 2020 · Nature Medicine · `sci_journal`
- **Type / stage:** `infrastructure` · `governance` · `methodological`
- **Contribution:** Extends SPIRIT for protocols evaluating interventions that include an AI component.
- **Why it matters:** Makes planned AI inputs, outputs, human-AI interactions and error handling explicit before a trial is run.
- **Limitations:** Protocol reporting cannot compensate for an inappropriate comparator, endpoint or deployment context.
- **Evidence boundary:** This is protocol-reporting guidance, not effectiveness evidence.
