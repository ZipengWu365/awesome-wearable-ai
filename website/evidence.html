What inclusion means

This is a selected research library, not an exhaustive systematic review. Research papers must meet the project's publication rules; official datasets, tools and standards have separate criteria. Preprints and unresolved entries remain outside the accepted count. Each accepted entry has one primary resource type. Its check date records when metadata were reviewed—not when all findings were independently verified.
