Quick definitions: digital twins, counterfactuals and evidence labels
Counterfactual
A “what if?” comparison: what might happen under a different action. Changing a model input alone does not prove a real treatment effect.
Digital twin
A model of a particular person or system, updated using observations. Check which signals it models and whether its simulations were tested.
Representation / features
A summary learned from signals that can be used for tasks such as activity recognition or risk prediction.
Policy value
The expected outcome of following a rule for choosing actions over time—not a government policy.
Clinical utility
Whether using a system improves patient or care outcomes, considering harms as well as benefits.
Review depth
How much curators checked: publication details, core metadata, or a study summary with limitations. This is not a score for scientific quality.
Signal abbreviations
ECG: electrical heart activity. PPG: optical pulse. EEG / MEG: electrical / magnetic brain activity. EMG: muscle activity. IMU: motion sensors. CGM: continuous glucose monitoring. PSG: a multi-signal sleep study.
Foundation model
A model pretrained for reuse across tasks. The library includes other model types too; not every model record is a foundation model.
