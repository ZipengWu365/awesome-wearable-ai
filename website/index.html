COLUMN 01

Predicting health changes

Wearable AI is learning more tasks from longer health records

A concrete gain: WearableQA reports 51.2% correct answers without Python tools and 71.3% with them, using the same GPT-5.4 model. Tool access and computing work differ.

Health actions and their effects

Health feedback is becoming more adaptive, but benefit must be tested

The wrist-wearable review finds more consistent benefits for physical activity than for many other health outcomes. The Long COVID messaging trial found no better main result than its comparison group.

Personal health models

Personal health models are being tested as parts of diabetes care

The 2026 study updates models weekly and sends daily feedback, but includes only 19 people. Earlier insulin-delivery work provides a separate trial example; it is not an October 2026 breakthrough.
