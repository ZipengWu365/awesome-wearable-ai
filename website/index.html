HOW THE FOUR AREAS RELATE

Recognise activity. Model health. Explore actions and personal simulation.

Starting point: human activity recognition

Inertial sensors identify actions such as walking, sitting and standing. These methods and datasets provide a foundation for studying behaviour and health.

Example: UCI HAR

    Estimate health states and future risk from body signals and personal records.

  2. 02 · INTERVENTIONWhat would change under a different action?

    Compare treatment choices and test whether adaptive support improves outcomes.

    Related work: actionable healthcare

    Update individual models and explore possible treatment responses.

    Example: twin-supported insulin care
04 · SMART WEARABLES & MIXED REALITYHow could the person see and interact with the model?

First-person sensing, body input and mixed reality provide potential interfaces. The collected work includes enabling technologies; a complete personal-twin interface is still a research goal.
