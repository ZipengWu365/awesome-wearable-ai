Small signals. Big possibilities.

01 · SENSE

Intelligence begins with small signals.

A wrist pulse. A change in gait. A night of sleep. Wearables turn continuous life into measurements—but every modality sees only part of the person.

Motion, ECG, EEG, PPG, EMG, glucose and sleep each require different assumptions.

02 · REPRESENT

Signals become machine-readable structure.

Models organise noisy streams into representations, patterns and predictions. The transformation is powerful; it is not the same as clinical truth.

  • calibrate
  • encode
  • learn
  • interpret

03 · THE LIFECYCLE

One model is only one moment in a longer loop.

Real-world impact depends on the chain around it: quality, measurement, prediction, causal reasoning, intervention, and learning from what happens next.

04 · THE ATLAS

A field this connected needs a map.

Six resource types form the atlas. The categories describe what a record is; the lifecycle describes where it helps. They are related, but not interchangeable.

05 · EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES

Prediction is not the end of the story.

An association does not guarantee prediction. A prediction does not establish a causal effect. A causal estimate does not automatically define a safe policy, and policy value does not guarantee clinical utility.

The atlas keeps these claim layers visible—especially when the distinctions are inconvenient.

A PAUSE FOR PROVENANCE

Evidence is not a badge. It is a boundary around what a record can legitimately support.

The registry separates evidence cards, verified metadata, and venue-screened records. It tracks whether a source was checked against a primary paper, official resource, or review reference. Coverage is curated—not claimed to be exhaustive.

This is a quality-screened scoping registry, not a PRISMA-exhaustive review. Scientific entries follow an explicit SCI/SCIE and archival-venue policy; canonical datasets, tools, standards and guidance use official-resource criteria. Pure preprints and unresolved venues stay in a transparent watchlist. Counts use one primary record type and are not mutually exclusive scientific concepts; verified_on records a metadata check, not a guarantee that no newer version exists.

BEYOND HAR & FOUNDATION MODELS

What is established.
What is still emerging.

The project maps research maturity as well as resources. Inclusion is not an endorsement of clinical readiness.

01 · ADAPT

Adaptive health systems

JITAIs, closed-loop therapeutics and remote monitoring linked to a defined response pathway.

Burden · latency · fail-safe behaviour · human override
02 · REASON

Causal & counterfactual AI

Time-varying effects, heterogeneous treatment response and policy evaluation beyond risk prediction.

Identification · positivity · uncertainty · external validity
03 · SIMULATE

World models & digital twins

Longitudinal latent states and patient-specific simulators—with calibration and intervention validity still unresolved.

Established → emerging → exploratory → speculative
04 · DEPLOY

Wearable-native systems

Multimodal agents, on-device sensing, synthetic data and cross-device or cross-population generalisation.

Privacy · battery · drift · transfer · safety
