# Roadmap

## v0.3.0 — audited public foundation

- machine-readable registry covering the complete sensing-to-intervention lifecycle
- strict scientific venue/source policy and explicit watchlist
- separate evidence depth, causal status and negative/null-result metadata
- generated README, CSV/JSON exports, family pages, coverage reports and search site

## v0.4 — primary-source deepening

- promote high-priority `venue_verified` entries to `metadata_verified` or full `evidence_card`
- add participant scale, pretraining scale, devices, sampling, tasks and external-validation fields where primary sources support them
- connect models to pretraining datasets, downstream datasets, tasks and reported evaluation protocols
- audit duplicated cohorts, dataset ancestry and potential train/test or pretraining contamination

## v0.5 — intervention evidence graph

- connect sensing variables, decision points, treatment options, tailoring variables and outcomes
- record trial estimands, comparator, follow-up, adherence, harms and null/negative findings
- distinguish micro-randomized proximal effects, full RCT outcomes, policy evaluation and deployed closed-loop control

## v0.6 — living curation

- scheduled link and metadata freshness checks
- release-diff reports and monthly Wearable AI Radar
- domain maintainers for motion, cardiac, neural, sleep, metabolic, causal methods and interventions
- archival snapshots with DOI-backed releases

## v1.0 — stable Wearable AI Evidence Atlas

- externally reviewed taxonomy and inclusion policy
- high-priority records primary-source audited
- searchable model × dataset × task × evidence graph
- contributor governance, correction process and stable release cadence
