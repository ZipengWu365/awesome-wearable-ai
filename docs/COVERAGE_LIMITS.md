# Coverage limits

## What this release covers

The registry maps six record types:

- models;
- datasets and cohorts;
- causal/counterfactual and adaptive-policy methods;
- digital measures and computational phenotypes;
- interventions and closed-loop systems;
- research infrastructure, standards and reporting guidance.

Model coverage includes motion/IMU, ECG, PPG, EEG/MEG, sleep/PSG, CGM/metabolic, respiratory/acoustic, multimodal physiology, sensor-language systems and general time-series enablers.

## What it does not claim

- exhaustive retrieval of every eligible paper;
- formal systematic-review completeness;
- independent reproduction of all reported results;
- full risk-of-bias assessment for every study;
- equivalence between venue prestige and evidence quality;
- clinical endorsement of listed models or devices;
- current regulatory authorization;
- legal advice about data access, privacy or medical-device status.

## Known under-covered areas

The following remain priority expansion areas:

- sweat, biochemical and microneedle sensing;
- textile, epidermal and implantable sensor foundation models;
- hearing, vestibular and spatial-audio wearables;
- maternal, neonatal and reproductive health;
- paediatric and low-resource populations;
- occupational sensing and worker safety;
- multimodal fusion involving imaging, EHR and genomics;
- fairness across skin tone, disability, age and geography;
- federated and privacy-preserving pretraining;
- prospective evaluations of sensor-language agents;
- closed-loop medication and care escalation beyond diabetes;
- long-horizon causal digital twins and policy learning;
- post-market monitoring and algorithm-version drift.

## Retrieval boundary

This is a curated living atlas assembled from primary publication pages, official reports, canonical resources and high-quality review/reference lists. The search log in `docs/SEARCH_STRATEGY.md` records the source families and screening rules. The date `verified_on` is a metadata-check date, not a guarantee that no newer version exists.

## Why counts should be interpreted cautiously

A paper can contribute a model, dataset and intervention, but the registry generally assigns one primary record type to avoid inflating totals. Model families also overlap: an ECG-language model is both an ECG model and a sensor-language system. Headline counts therefore represent records, not mutually exclusive scientific concepts.
